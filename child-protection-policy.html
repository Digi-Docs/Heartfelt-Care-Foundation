Heartfelt Care Foundation is committed to the safety, dignity, and wellbeing of every child we work with.

  • Photos and stories involving children are shared only with the consent of a parent, guardian, or responsible caregiver.
  • Children are never portrayed in a way that exposes their exact location, full identity details, or that compromises their dignity.
  • All staff, volunteers, and partners are expected to treat children with respect and report any safeguarding concerns immediately.
  • We welcome feedback or concerns from the public regarding our child protection practices at heartfeltcarefoundation@gmail.com.
