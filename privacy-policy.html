Heartfelt Care Foundation ("we," "us," "our") respects the privacy of everyone who visits this website, registers as a member, volunteers, donates, or contacts us.

Information We Collect

Contact details you provide via forms (name, phone number, email address); membership registration details submitted through our Google Form; comments or messages submitted through the website; and basic technical data (browser type, pages visited) for site performance purposes.

How We Use Your Information

To respond to inquiries, comments, and membership applications; to keep you informed about programs, events, and ways to get involved; and to process donations and send confirmation receipts. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Children's Information

Photos and stories of children supported by the Foundation are shared only with appropriate consent from guardians or caregivers, and in a manner that protects each child's dignity and safety. See our Child Protection Policy for more detail.

Data Storage & Security

Information submitted through our forms is stored securely and accessed only by authorized team members.

Your Rights

You may request to see, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you by contacting us at heartfeltcarefoundation@gmail.com.
