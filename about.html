About Heartfelt Care Foundation

Our story, our founder, and the vision, mission and values that guide everything we do.

Meet Our Founder & CEO

Generorodgers Vincent Sylivesta is the Founder and CEO of Heartfelt Care Foundation. For him, this Foundation is more than an organization. It is a personal commitment to serve people, restore hope, and make a meaningful difference in the lives of those who need support.

He believes that caring for others should not only be something we talk about, but something we put into action. His passion is rooted in the belief that every child deserves hope, every vulnerable person deserves dignity, and every community can become stronger when people choose to care for one another.

Through Heartfelt Care Foundation, he is building a community where compassion, unity, and service bring people together to create real and lasting impact. He is passionate about connecting people with opportunities to help, contribute their talents, support one another, and become part of something bigger than themselves.

Message from the CEO

Welcome to Heartfelt Care Foundation.

At Heartfelt Care Foundation, we believe that every person deserves dignity, compassion, hope, and an opportunity for a better future. Our commitment is to transform compassion into meaningful action by supporting orphaned children, vulnerable individuals, and underserved communities.

Through collaboration, service, and community participation, we seek to create sustainable impact and build stronger, more caring communities.

We invite individuals, partners, volunteers, and well-wishers to join us in this journey of restoring smiles, rebuilding hope, and changing lives.

Generorodgers Vincent Sylivesta
Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Vision & Mission

Vision

To see a society where every orphan and disadvantaged person lives with dignity, hope, happiness, access to basic needs, and the opportunity to build a better future.

Mission

To restore hope and improve the quality of life of orphans and disadvantaged people by providing psychological and social support, education, healthcare, safe living conditions, and opportunities for economic empowerment through partnerships with communities, professionals, government, and other organizations.

Core Purpose

To care, empower, and create opportunities for vulnerable people so they can live dignified, meaningful, and independent lives.

Main Objectives

  • Psychological Well-beingProvide counseling and psychological support.
  • Education EmpowermentSupport education and help students learn under favorable conditions.
  • Safe LivingSupport safe housing and better living environments.
  • Economic IndependenceReduce dependency through skills development and income-generating opportunities.
  • Community AwarenessPromote inclusion and reduce discrimination against vulnerable groups.
